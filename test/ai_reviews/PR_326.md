# PR326 review: switch the ideal burn at its window's ends between integration steps

## Scope

Review of the SpaceAGORA `burn-window` branch against its base,
`actuator-followups` (PR #325). One commit: the ideal burn of
`BaseThrusterModel` switches at its window's ends through the burning flag
and the window-edge callback that the mounted burn of #325 uses. The PI asked
for the fix on 2026-10-15 after the defect was found while building #325.
This PR changes the results of runs with ideal burns.

Authorship disclosure: this record was drafted by the author of the PR. The
plan assigns behaviour PRs of the actuator layer an independent reviewer (the
PI, or the second reviewer when available); this record does not replace that
review.

## Changed Files

- `src/models/gnc/control/propulsive_maneuvers.jl`: `_burn_active` (interior times by the clock, the ends by the flag) in the ideal force and mass flow; `refresh_burn!` for both modes, its mounted part in `_refresh_mounted_engine!`; `burn_edge` for both modes; the hook docstring.
- `src/models/vehicle/actuators/thruster_models.jl`: docstring and state comment for both modes.
- `src/orchestration/simulation/callbacks/control_callbacks.jl`: the window-edge callback for every burn scheduler; the schedule's initialization marks the first derivative discontinuous when a burn is already on.

Outside `src/`: tests.

## Findings

No P1 or P2 findings. Points a reviewer should check:

- By design, results change: every ideal burn now lasts exactly its window.
  The measured differences (PR description) are 0.06 s to 0.43 s of burn
  per burn on main, a few metres of semi-major axis.
- Low, semantics: direct calls of `calcControlForceTorque` and
  `calcControlMassFlowRate` exactly at a window's end now return the burn's
  state (the flag) rather than "on"; at every other time they are unchanged.
  Suite 05 checks both ends with the flag in each state.
- Low: if the solver cannot stop at a window's end (a stop beyond the
  registered stops), the ends are not landed and the burn switches inside a
  step, as before the fix.
- Low: the window-edge condition compares times exactly; the stops and the
  window are the same Float64 values, so a landed end always matches.

## P1 Assessment

No P1 findings. The change is limited to the ideal burn's two ends; runs
without an ideal burn are bitwise unchanged against #325 (the Apollo
fingerprint, the momentum-manager example, the walkthrough), and mounted
burns already switched this way.
