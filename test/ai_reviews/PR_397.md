# Review record: PR 397: Apollo descent engine trim

## Scope

Review of the SpaceAGORA `apollo-engine-trim` branch against main
(BASE_PLACEHOLDER). A follow-up of the thruster gimbals (#346): the Apollo
descent controller can trim the descent engine with its gimbals in mounted
actuation. Two commits: the trim with its tests and docs, then a test of a
single control cycle's solve.

Authorship disclosure: this record was drafted by the author of the PR. The
plan assigns behaviour PRs of the actuator layer an independent reviewer (the
PI, or the second reviewer when available); this record does not replace that
review.

## Changed Files

- `src/models/gnc/control/landing/apollo_descent_control.jl`: `engine_trim` in `ApolloDescentControlConfig` (refused unless `actuation=:mounted`); `_trim_engine_gimbals!`, called at the end of `_update_descent_thruster_levels!`, sets the descent engine's gimbal levels to the angles that put its thrust line through the body origin; `_best_axis_angle` and `_turn_about` (the one-axis solve and the gimbal turn, Rodrigues).

Outside `src/`: the lunar landing page, tests.

## Findings

No P1 or P2 findings. Points a reviewer should check:

- Low, by design: the centre of mass is the body origin, as in the
  dynamics (torques about it, a fixed inertia); a trim law aimed at a moving
  centre of mass would need the dynamics to move it first.
- Low: the angles are solved one gimbal at a time from the current angles,
  twelve alternating passes for two gimbals; the engine's own gimbal limits
  then bound the command, so a trim beyond them leaves a residual torque the
  RCS loop carries.
- Low: the jets' allocation layout keeps the nominal engine direction (it is
  cached at the first cycle); with the engine trimmed its torque is zero, so
  the allocation is unaffected.
- Low, known gap: the plume-surface model takes the exhaust along body +z
  and ignores the trim tilt.

## P1 Assessment

No P1 findings. Without `engine_trim` the trim function is not called (the
config flag guards the call), so the default mounted and ideal paths are
unchanged; the four fingerprints are bitwise unchanged against main.

## Tests Added/Updated

- `test/unit/gnc/apollo_descent_tests.jl`: `engine_trim` refused with `:ideal` actuation; with the descent engine 0.05 m off its axis, a rate-limited single-axis gimbal reaches `atan(0.05, 1.5)` and the engine's torque about the centre of mass vanishes; trim off, the gimbal holds; two gimbals trimmed in a run; from zero angles, a single control cycle's solve with two gimbals leaves no torque (one alternating pass leaves 1.7e-5 N m).
