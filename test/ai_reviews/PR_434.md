(80b53030f). The first PR of the interlinks track
(`_Command_Center/SpaceAGORA_Constellations_and_Interlinks_Plan_2026-10-19.md`,

## Findings

No P1 or P2 findings.

Points a reviewer should check:

- **Bitwise identity.** The laser force on the second endpoint is the negation
  of the first's: `(B·P/c)·((r_a − r_b)/d)` negated equals `(B·P/c)·((r_b − r_a)/d)`
  bit for bit, since subtraction is antisymmetric and negation exact. The force
  sums run in the same edge order. Both examples and the unchanged interlink
  tests confirm it.
- **Low, by design: endpoint order.** Link endpoints are passed in the key's
  order (the lower `(spacecraft, terminal)` first), not registration order. The
  laser link is symmetric. Directed links (power, data) come with I-2, which
  stores direction.
- **Low: per-edge dispatch.** Edges hold `AbstractInterlink` values, so
  `link_force` is dispatched per edge (a union of concrete kinds when mixed).
  The laser runs' results are unchanged; their timing is not part of this
  record.
- **Low: alias names cannot warn.** `ConstellationInterLinkModel` and
  `InterLinkParameters` are constant aliases of the new types, so `isa` and
  type identity keep working. A call by the alias name alone cannot warn; their
  old keywords and fields do, and their docstrings open with "Deprecated, use".
- **Low: naming left for I-6.** `InterLinkGraph`, `InterLinkKey`,
  `InterLinkHistory`, `edge_properties` and the `laser_dv` and `laser_delta_sma`
  diagnostics keep their names; I-6 renames the outputs and harmonizes the rest.
- **Merge order with #421.** #421 (0.5.0, open) renames `SchedulingPolicyModel`'s
  `target_idx` in the same scheduling file, and the checkpoint identity stores
  `target_idx`. Whichever lands second merges main; the version 1 mapping
  carries the configuration's other fields unchanged.
